Types of Medical Records

Different types of medical records help maintain a useful history of your pet's healthcare.

Vaccination Records

Maintain information about vaccines received and recommended future vaccination visits.

General Health Check-ups

Record routine veterinary examinations and important health observations.

Diagnosis Records

Keep information about health conditions identified by a qualified veterinarian.

Treatment Records

Maintain details about treatments and care provided during veterinary visits.

Medication Records

Organize medication information and instructions provided by the veterinarian.

Follow-up Records

Keep track of follow-up visits and veterinary recommendations.

Importance of Maintaining Medical Records

1. Health History

Medical records provide a history of previous veterinary visits, health conditions and treatments.

2. Vaccination Tracking

Organized vaccination information helps pet owners discuss appropriate vaccination schedules with veterinarians.

3. Better Communication

Accurate records help pet owners communicate previous health information to veterinary professionals.

4. Follow-up Care

Recording follow-up dates helps owners keep track of veterinary recommendations and future visits.

Tips for Responsible Pet Healthcare

  • Schedule regular veterinary check-ups.
  • Maintain vaccination information.
  • Keep previous prescriptions and medical reports organized.
  • Follow the veterinarian's instructions for treatment.
  • Record important changes in your pet's health.
  • Attend recommended follow-up appointments.

Medical Record Management

The Vyshnavi Pet Care Management System is designed to organize medical information associated with registered pets.

Medical records can be linked with pet profiles and owner details to maintain a structured history of veterinary care.

This helps keep important information organized and easier to refer to during future veterinary visits.
