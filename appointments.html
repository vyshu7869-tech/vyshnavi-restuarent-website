Types of Veterinary Appointments

Different appointments help address different pet health and care requirements.

General Check-up

Routine health examinations help monitor your pet's general health and well-being.

Vaccination

Veterinary visits help maintain vaccination records and follow the recommended vaccination schedule.

Dental Check-up

Dental consultations help assess your pet's oral health and discuss suitable dental care.

Follow-up Visit

Follow-up appointments help monitor progress after a previous consultation or treatment.

Grooming Consultation

Grooming consultations help discuss coat care, hygiene and suitable grooming routines.

Other Consultation

Other veterinary consultations can be recorded according to your pet's individual needs.

Before Your Appointment

1. Keep Pet Information Ready

Make sure your pet's registration and owner details are accurate.

2. Carry Previous Medical Records

Bring relevant vaccination details, prescriptions and previous medical records when appropriate.

3. Explain Your Concerns

Make a note of any changes in your pet's behaviour or health that you would like to discuss with the veterinarian.

4. Confirm Appointment Details

Check the appointment date and time with the veterinary clinic.

Why Regular Veterinary Visits Matter

Regular veterinary visits help monitor the health of pets and provide opportunities to discuss preventive care.

Veterinarians can assess health concerns, recommend suitable care and maintain important medical information.

Keeping appointment details organized also helps pet owners remember scheduled visits and follow-up consultations.

Appointment Management

The Vyshnavi Pet Care Management System is designed to organize appointment information, including owner details, pet details, appointment dates, timings and reasons for visits.

These records can be connected with pet profiles and medical records to support organized veterinary administration.
